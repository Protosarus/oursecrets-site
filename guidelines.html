Community Guidelines

Last updated: July 4, 2026

OurSecrets is a place for pseudonymous personal expression. Your nickname may be displayed publicly with your content, so avoid choosing one that reveals sensitive personal information. These Community Guidelines explain what is welcome on the platform and what is not. They work together with our Terms of Service and Child Safety Standards.

Allowed

  • Pseudonymous personal experiences
  • Feelings, confessions, concerns, and opinions
  • Respectful discussion
  • Non-identifying personal stories

Not Allowed

  • Credible threats or plans of violence
  • Serious criminal facilitation
  • Child exploitation or sexualized content involving minors
  • Harassment or hate speech
  • Doxxing or sharing private identifying information about others
  • Impersonation
  • Fraud or scams
  • Illegal transactions
  • Graphic prohibited content
  • Spam or duplicate content
  • Vote or report manipulation
  • Malicious false reports

Platform-Generated Content

OurSecrets may contain platform-generated or AI-generated seed content used to support the Service. Such content is not necessarily a submission from a real user.

Community Voting

Believe and Doubt votes help the community evaluate content and express sentiment. Community voting is not a legal determination of truth and does not replace moderation or reporting. Voting should reflect genuine community judgment, not coordinated manipulation.

Reports

Use in-app reports for content that appears to violate these Guidelines, our Terms, or applicable law. Reports help our team and systems review potential violations. Do not submit false or malicious reports to harass others or suppress legitimate content.

Immediate Threats and Emergencies

If you see a credible immediate threat or emergency, contact the appropriate emergency services in your location. OurSecrets is not an emergency response service and cannot dispatch help on your behalf.

Enforcement

Content that violates these Guidelines may be hidden, reviewed, restricted, or permanently removed through reports, moderation, or community voting. Repeated or serious violations may result in account restrictions or deletion.

OurSecrets does not verify or guarantee that user-submitted secrets are true. Community discussion and voting reflect user opinion, not verified fact.

Contact

Questions about these Guidelines: support@oursecretsapp.com.
