Community Guidelines

OurSecrets is intended to be a safe place for anonymous expression.

Allowed

  • Sharing personal thoughts
  • Anonymous confessions
  • Opinions and experiences

Not Allowed

  • Harassment or bullying
  • Threats or violent content
  • Hate speech
  • Sharing private information about others
  • Illegal activity

Violating content may be removed and repeated violations may lead to account restrictions.
