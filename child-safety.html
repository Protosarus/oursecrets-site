Child Safety Standards

Last updated: July 4, 2026

OurSecrets is a user-generated content platform. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Prohibited content and conduct

  • Child sexual abuse and exploitation
  • Grooming, solicitation, or sexual exploitation of minors
  • Sexualized content involving minors
  • Requests for such content
  • Sharing, distributing, or promoting child exploitation material
  • Any content that facilitates or promotes CSAE

Enforcement

We remove violating content, restrict or terminate offending accounts, and may preserve relevant information where required for safety, legal compliance, or reporting to appropriate authorities.

We may report suspected CSAM or CSAE to appropriate authorities as required or permitted by applicable law.

Reporting

If you encounter content that may involve child exploitation, report it immediately through the in-app report feature. You may also contact support@oursecretsapp.com.

If you believe a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services and the appropriate local authorities in your jurisdiction without delay.

Age policy

OurSecrets is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child is using the Service or you have identified CSAE or CSAM, report it immediately through the app and contact local authorities when necessary.

Contact

Child safety concerns: support@oursecretsapp.com
