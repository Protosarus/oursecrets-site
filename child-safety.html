Child Safety Standards (CSAE/CSAM)

OurSecrets is a user-generated content (UGC) platform. We have a zero-tolerance policy for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Prohibited content

  • Any CSAM or content that sexualizes minors
  • Grooming, solicitation, or sexual exploitation of minors
  • Any content that promotes or facilitates CSAE

Enforcement

  • We remove violating content and ban offending accounts.
  • We may report suspected CSAM/CSAE to appropriate authorities as required by law.

Reporting

Users can report content in-app. For urgent reports or legal requests, contact: sirinumit@gmail.com

Age policy

OurSecrets is intended for adults. If you believe a child is using the service or you have identified CSAE/CSAM, please report it immediately.
